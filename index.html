Parakeet Keys

A keyboard for iPhone that turns your speech into text on the phone itself. Tap the mic, talk, and the words appear in the app you are typing in.

  • Speech recognition runs on your iPhone with NVIDIA's Parakeet model. Your voice is not sent anywhere.
  • A full keyboard with swipe typing that learns from the swipes you correct.
  • Optional cleanup of dictated text with Apple's on-device model, on iPhones with Apple Intelligence.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics.
