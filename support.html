Support

How to set up Parakeet Keys, what to expect the first time, and fixes for common problems.

Set up the keyboard

  1. Open Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard, and choose Parakeet.
  2. Tap Parakeet in the list and turn on Allow Full Access.
  3. In any app, touch and hold the globe key and choose Parakeet.

Why Full Access: iOS doesn't let keyboards use the microphone, so the Parakeet Keys app does the listening and hands the text to the keyboard. Full Access lets the two share that text through storage on your iPhone. Nothing leaves the phone; see the privacy policy.

The first run

The first time you dictate, the app downloads the speech model, about 600 MB, so use Wi-Fi if you can. iOS then optimizes the model for your iPhone, which takes a few minutes. Don't force-quit the app while this runs. It happens only once.

Dictating

  1. Tap the mic key. The Parakeet Keys app opens and starts a session.
  2. Go back to the app you were typing in: tap its name in the top-left corner, or swipe right along the bottom edge.
  3. Talk, then tap the mic key again. The text appears at your cursor.

Why the orange dot stays on

iOS shows an orange dot whenever an app has the microphone open. During a session, the app keeps the microphone open in the background so it can start listening as soon as you tap the mic key. It only keeps audio between your two taps, and discards it after transcribing. The dot goes away when the session ends.

End a session

Tap End session in the app, or in the Live Activity on the Lock Screen or in the Dynamic Island. A session also ends by itself after 5 minutes without dictation.

Troubleshooting

The keyboard asks me to turn on Full Access.
Go to Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Parakeet and turn on Allow Full Access.

The download stopped.
Open the app and tap Retry. Files that already finished downloading are kept.

It has been preparing the model for a long time.
The first optimization can take several minutes. If it's still running after 15 minutes, restart your iPhone and open the app again.

I talked, but no text appeared.
Very short or silent recordings produce no text. Make sure a call, Siri or another app isn't using the microphone, then try again.

Parakeet isn't in the globe-key list.
Add it again under Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards.

Swipe picked the wrong word.
Tap the word you meant in the bar above the keys. The keyboard remembers the correction. To clear what it learned, use Forget Learned Corrections in the app's settings.

I can't find the cleanup options.
They need iOS 26 or later and an iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on.
