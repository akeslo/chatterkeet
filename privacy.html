Privacy policy

Effective 18 October 2026

Chatterkeet does not collect any data about you. What it handles stays on your iPhone.

Your voice

When you dictate, the Chatterkeet app records from the microphone and turns the audio into text on your iPhone. The audio is kept in memory only until it has been transcribed, then discarded. It is never saved to a file and never sent off the device.

Network use

The app uses the internet for one thing: downloading the speech model (about 600 MB) from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) the first time you dictate. Hugging Face sees the normal details of a download request, such as your IP address. No audio, text or personal information is sent. After the download, dictation works offline.

What you type

The keyboard does not send your keystrokes or text anywhere. It contains no network code.

iOS asks you to turn on Allow Full Access for the keyboard. Chatterkeet needs it for one reason: so the keyboard and the app can share data through storage on your iPhone (an App Group) that only they can read. That shared data is:

  • the text of your latest dictation, so the keyboard can insert it;
  • whether a dictation is in progress, and any error message;
  • your keyboard settings and text shortcuts;
  • the swipe corrections the keyboard has learned from you. You can clear these in the app's settings.

On-device AI features

Some optional features use Apple's Foundation Models, the language model built into iOS with Apple Intelligence, which runs on your iPhone. Chatterkeet uses it to clean up or rewrite dictated text when you turn that on, and to choose between likely words when a swipe is ambiguous. You can turn either off in the app.

What we don't do

There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and no third-party code that collects data. We don't sell or share data, because we don't have any.

Deleting your data

Delete the Chatterkeet app from your iPhone. That removes the downloaded model, your settings, shortcuts, learned words and any text the keyboard and app shared. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
